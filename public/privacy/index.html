Privacy and account deletion

Signing in

When you choose Google, Facebook or GitHub, the provider receives your sign-in request. Side A uses your provider account identifier, name, email and available profile image to create and recognize your account. The server stores the account association and authentication data in Cloudflare D1. A first-party cookie keeps you signed in.

Your diary

Private notes are visible only in your signed-in diary. You choose which entries to share, whether to publish your profile, and which statistics and badges appear there. Anyone with access to a published page can read or copy it.

When you look up metadata, the service sends the episode link to Spotify or YouTube. Podcast searches send the search term to Podcast Index or Apple. Opening a source link or loading provider artwork connects your browser to the publisher or image host.

Export or delete your account

  1. Sign in using the provider you originally used for Side A.
  2. Open Profile settings. Choose Export my data if you want a JSON copy of your diary.
  3. Choose Delete account, type DELETE, then confirm permanent deletion. If prompted, sign out and sign back in first.

Deletion removes your live profile, diary entries, provider association and sessions. Copies in infrastructure backups expire on the host's retention schedule. If you removed Side A in Facebook settings first, reconnect with the same Facebook account to use the deletion control.
