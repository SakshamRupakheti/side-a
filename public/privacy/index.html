Privacy and account deletion

Signing in

When you choose Google, Facebook or GitHub, the provider receives your sign-in request. suni. uses your provider account identifier, name, email and available profile image to create and recognize your account. The server stores the account association and authentication data in Cloudflare D1. A first-party cookie keeps you signed in.

Your diary

Personal descriptions, reflections and private notes are visible only in your signed-in diary. A favorite moment appears publicly only if you share that moment, share its entry and publish your profile. You choose which entries to share, whether to publish your profile, and which statistics and badges appear there. Anyone with access to a published page can read or copy it.

When you look up metadata, the service sends the link or identifier to Spotify, YouTube or a supported publisher. If you start a draft before signing in, this browser tab temporarily keeps it during sign-in. Podcast searches send the search term to Podcast Index or Apple. Opening a source link or loading provider artwork connects your browser to the publisher or image host.

Social posts

Publishing a post is a separate choice. Its profile, diary entry and post must all be public. You can include the public takeaway and individually shared moments. Bookmarks are private. Likes contribute to a count. Reports go to the operator. Blocking removes social interactions between signed-in accounts. Signed-out visitors can still read public pages. Downloads and copies held by others can outlast unpublishing.

Listening Wall and Fresh Voices

Fresh Voices lists listeners by their latest public postcard. Sharing badges count distinct podcast postcards, shows and captions or takeaways you chose to publish. Your profile settings have a separate switch to join the ranked Listening Wall. Leaving the ranking takes effect when you save. Unpublishing a postcard removes its contribution to both lists.

To report a postcard or listener, open Safety options, choose Report and describe the concern. An operator can review the report and hide the post or profile. Your export includes your own reports. Closing a report retains it for review. Deleting the associated post, reported account or reporting account removes the corresponding report.

Listening history imports

Your browser reads Spotify and YouTube export files locally. After you confirm, suni. stores the selected episode titles, creators, provider IDs, and available activity counts, dates and durations in your private diary. You can export these summaries or delete their entries. Saved reviews and unfinished import selections stay in this browser until you discard them or finish importing. Clearing site data removes local drafts.

Export or delete your account

  1. Sign in using the provider you originally used for suni.
  2. Open Profile settings. Choose Export my data if you want a JSON copy of your diary.
  3. Choose Delete account, type DELETE, then confirm permanent deletion. If prompted, sign out and sign back in first.

Deletion removes your live profile, diary entries, provider association and sessions. Cloudflare D1 Free can retain a recoverable copy for seven days. An operator-created encrypted backup can retain earlier records until that snapshot expires. If you removed suni. in Facebook settings first, reconnect with the same Facebook account to use the deletion control.
